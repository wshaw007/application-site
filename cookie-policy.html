What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, local storage and scripts, which we refer to collectively as "cookies".

Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies Needed for the website to operate — for example, to load pages correctly, remember your progress through a form, or keep the site secure. These cannot be switched off.

Analytics cookies Help us understand how visitors use our website — which pages are visited, how long people stay and where they drop off — so we can improve the experience.

Advertising and measurement cookies Used to measure the performance of our advertising campaigns, attribute registrations and purchases to the correct campaign, and show you relevant ads on other platforms.

Functional cookies Remember choices you make, such as a selected webinar time or form entries, so you don't have to enter them again.

How long cookies last

Some cookies are deleted when you close your browser (session cookies). Others remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them (persistent cookies), typically between a few days and two years depending on the cookie.

How to manage cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies and alert you when a cookie is being set. Note that blocking essential cookies may stop parts of our website from working.

Useful links: - Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647 - Safari: support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471 - Firefox: support.mozilla.org/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop - Edge: support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge

You can also opt out of personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com and from Meta through your Facebook ad preferences.

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies via a cookie banner, and you can change your choices at any time.
