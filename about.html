About Your Free Masterclass

Your Free Masterclass is an online education and training brand focused on helping people better understand digital products, online business systems, marketing and emerging AI tools.

Through live training sessions and educational programs, we provide practical information designed to help participants develop their knowledge and skills in online business.

What we teach

Our training covers how to set up and operate an online business built around digital products, including:

  • Using pre-built (done-for-you) sales funnels and landing pages
  • Driving traffic through paid advertising and organic channels
  • Email marketing and follow-up systems
  • Using AI tools to create content, copy and marketing assets faster
  • Reselling digital training and software as an affiliate or licensee

How it works

Most people start with our free live training. It's an educational session — you'll leave with a clear understanding of the model, the tools and what's involved. For those who want to go further, we offer a paid training program and software package that is explained in full during the session.

Our approach

We believe in showing the work. Our training is based on systems we use in our own business, taught step by step, with the tools and templates included. We don't promise outcomes — starting a business takes effort, time and resources, and results depend on the individual. What we do promise is clear, practical education.
